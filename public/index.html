How it Works

Follow these three simple steps to ensure your voice is heard in this year's general elections.

badge

1. Log in with your matric number arrow_forward

Use the same credentials you use on the university portal. Your account and ballot are tied to your matric number.
how_to_vote

2. Pick your candidates arrow_forward

Your ballot shows only the positions relevant to your college and department. Select one per position and review before submitting.
task_alt

3. Submit — you're done arrow_forward

Once submitted, your vote is recorded and your matric number is marked as voted. You cannot vote again with the same account.

Uncompromising Security

  • check_circle

    One vote per matric number

    Once you submit, your matric number is locked. You cannot vote again with the same account.

  • check_circle

    Your choice stays anonymous

    We record that you voted — but not who you voted for. Even admins can only see totals, not individual selections.

  • check_circle

    No editing after submission

    Votes are final. They cannot be changed or deleted — not by you, not by the system administrator.
