Official Voting Rules & Guidelines

Please read carefully before participating in the SUG General Elections. Violation of these rules may result in the disqualification of your vote.

verified_user

1. Voter Eligibility

  • You must be a fully registered student for the current academic session.
  • You must possess a valid Matriculation Number recognized by the University Registry.
  • All tuition and union dues must be cleared prior to the election date.
exposure_plus_1

2. One Student, One Vote Policy

  • Each eligible student is entitled to only ONE ballot submission across all devices.
  • Attempting to log in with multiple accounts or sharing credentials with others is strictly prohibited.
  • The system will automatically flag and nullify duplicate votes originating from unauthorized sessions.
gavel

3. Electoral Code of Conduct

  • No Campaigning on Election Day: All forms of physical and digital campaigning must cease 24 hours before polls open.
  • No Intimidation: Voters must not be pressured, harassed, or coerced into voting for specific candidates.
  • Integrity: Candidates found bribing voters or manipulating the platform will be immediately disqualified.
report

4. Penalties & Disqualification

  • Violation of any election rules will result in disciplinary action by the University Senate.
  • Fraudulent voting attempts will lead to immediate account suspension and a potential academic strike.
  • If you notice any suspicious activity or violations, please report it immediately to the Electoral Commission.

By logging in to vote, you acknowledge and agree to abide by all the rules stated above.
