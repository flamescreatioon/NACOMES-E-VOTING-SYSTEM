Official Privacy Policy

DOCUMENT REF: MOUAU-ELEC-PP-2026

ISSUING AUTHORITY: MOUAU Electoral Commission

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2026

STATUS: Active / Binding

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the Michael Okpara University of Agriculture, Umudike (MOUAU) Student Union Electoral Commission collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from students using the online voting portal.

I. Information We Collect

To verify eligibility and facilitate the voting process, we collect and process the following information during your session:

  1. Authentication Data: Matriculation number and portal password (used strictly for authentication against the University's active directory).
  2. Demographic Data: Your enrolled college and department to determine which specific college-level elections you are eligible for.
  3. Session Data: IP addresses and browser fingerprints are temporarily collected to prevent duplicate votes and mitigate fraudulent attacks.

II. How We Protect Your Ballot (Anonymity Guarantee)

Ensuring that your vote remains completely secret is the cornerstone of this platform's architecture:

  1. Decoupling of Identity: The moment you cast your vote, your identity is verified, but the actual ballot data is encrypted and saved independently. There is no database link connecting your matriculation number to the candidates you selected.
  2. Zero-Knowledge Encryption: Our databases cannot be reverse-engineered to discover who you voted for, even by the database administrators or the Electoral Commission.

III. Data Retention

We do not store your data longer than necessary for the purpose of the election:

  • We never store your university portal passwords on our servers. They are passed securely via HTTPS to the central authentication service and immediately discarded.
  • Anonymized vote counts and election results are kept indefinitely for historical records.
  • All IP addresses and security logs are purged 30 days after the official election results are ratified.

IV. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. Aggregate, anonymized voter turnout statistics (such as the percentage of students who voted per faculty) may be shared publicly.

Signed and Ratified

The MOUAU Electoral Commission Board
