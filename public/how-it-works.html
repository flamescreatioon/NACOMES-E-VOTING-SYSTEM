Voting Process Explained

Learn everything you need to know about the 2026 NACOME Elections (Department of Computer Engineering, CEET — Michael Okpara University of Agriculture, Umudike), from authentication to result verification.

1. Secure Authentication

To ensure only eligible students can participate, we use a robust authentication system linked to the University Registry.

  • Matriculation Number: You must use your official matric number in the format MOUAU/CME/[Year]/[Number] (e.g. MOUAU/CME/17/9962).
  • Secure Password: Use your portal password. If you haven't changed it, use the default provided by your department.
  • Identity Protection: Your login session is encrypted. Once logged in, your identity is detached from your actual ballot to ensure 100% anonymity.

2. Casting Your Ballot

Our voting dashboard is designed to be intuitive and transparent.

  • NACOME Executive Positions: Vote for NACOME President, Vice-President, Secretary-General, Assistant Secretary-General, Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Public Relations Officer (PRO), Director of Welfare, Director of Sports, Director of Socials, and Technical & Academic Director.
  • Manifesto Review: Click on any candidate to read their official manifesto and vision statement before selecting.
  • One Vote Rule: You can only submit your ballot once. The system will block any multi-vote attempts using advanced session tracking.
  • Review Before Submission: You'll have a chance to review all your selections before final submission.

3. Receipt & Verification

After voting, you receive confirmation that your voice has been recorded.

  • Digital Receipt: An encrypted transaction ID is generated for your vote. This allows the Commission to verify the total count without knowing who voted for whom.
  • Tamper-Proof Ledger: All votes are recorded on a secure ledger that prevents any alteration after the ballot is cast.
  • Real-time Results: You can view live voter turnout and aggregated results as they come in on the official Results page.
