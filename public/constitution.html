Student Union Constitution

The supreme law guiding the activities, governance, and rights of students within the Student Union Government.

PREAMBLE

We, the students of the University, firmly and solemnly resolved to live in unity and harmony as one indivisible entity dedicated to the pursuit of academic excellence, defense of students' rights, and the general welfare of the student body, do hereby make, enact, and give to ourselves this Constitution.

ARTICLE 1: NAME AND SUPREMACY

Section 1: Name

The union shall be known and addressed as the Student Union Government (SUG), hereinafter referred to as "The Union".

Section 2: Supremacy of the Constitution

This Constitution is supreme and its provisions shall have binding force on all authorities and persons throughout the Student Union Government. If any other union law, directive, or regulation is inconsistent with the provisions of this Constitution, this Constitution shall prevail, and that other law shall, to the extent of the inconsistency, be void.

ARTICLE 2: AIMS AND OBJECTIVES

  • To protect and defend the rights and interests of all students of the University.
  • To foster unity, peace, and mutual understanding among students.
  • To serve as the recognized representative body of the students in all matters affecting them within and outside the University.
  • To promote the intellectual, social, and cultural welfare of the students.

ARTICLE 3: THE EXECUTIVE COUNCIL

The Executive powers of the Union shall be vested in the Executive Council which shall comprise:

  1. The President: Chief Executive Officer of the Union, presides over all Executive Council meetings, and represents the Union in all University Senate and Management meetings.
  2. The Vice President: Assists the President and coordinates the activities of all standing committees.
  3. The General Secretary: Chief administrative officer, responsible for correspondence, record-keeping, and taking minutes at all Executive meetings.
  4. The Financial Secretary: Responsible for receiving and recording all monies, and managing the financial records of the Union.
  5. The Public Relations Officer (PRO): Responsible for disseminating information, managing the image of the Union, and handling press releases.

ARTICLE 4: ELECTIONS AND TENURE

Section 1: Electoral Process

All Executive Council members shall be elected democratically by the general student body via a secret ballot system (or an approved electronic voting portal). The Electoral Commission shall be an independent body appointed by the University Management and the outgoing Legislative Council.

Section 2: Tenure of Office

The tenure of all elected Executive Council members shall be for one academic session. No student shall hold the same executive office for more than one tenure.
